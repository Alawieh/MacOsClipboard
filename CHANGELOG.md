# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-14

First public release.

- Clipboard history opened with ⌘⇧V (Win+Shift+V on a PC keyboard), shown at the mouse pointer.
- Records text (with formatting), images, animated GIFs, video, audio and Finder files.
- Pastes straight into the app you were using (needs Accessibility permission; otherwise copies).
- Search, pin, delete, Clear All; ↑ ↓ ↩ and ⌘1–⌘9 keyboard control.
- Menu-bar app with Launch at Login, on by default after installing to Applications.
- Skips passwords marked secret by password managers; never reads your folders; no network access.
